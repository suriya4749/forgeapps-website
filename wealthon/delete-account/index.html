Delete your WealthOn data

WealthOn by Forge Apps · Last updated 30 October 2026

WealthOn has no accounts. Everything you enter is stored only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time, straight away.

Delete everything

  1. Open WealthOn.
  2. Go to Settings → Erase all data.
  3. Confirm. Everything is removed from your phone immediately.

Uninstalling WealthOn also removes all of its data from the phone.

Delete single items

Delete any entry in the app. Deleted items stay in Recently deleted for 30 days so you can undo a mistake, then they are removed for good.

Data sent to the optional smart features

When you agree to use a smart feature, the request is used only to produce the answer and is not stored, so there is nothing to delete. The shared shop memory holds only shop names and their usual category, with nothing that links to you. If you want a shop name removed from it, email support@forgeapps.in with the subject "WealthOn data".

Your own backups and exports

Backup files, CSV exports and PDF reports you saved or shared (for example to your Google Drive) are copies you control. Delete them where you saved them. If Android's own device backup is on for your Google account, you can manage it in Android settings.
