What it does

Money, made simple.

Built for people juggling a salary, bills and EMIs, with a short setup that doesn't wear you out.

01 · Safe to spend

One number for today.

WealthOn sets aside your bills, EMIs and savings first, then tells you exactly what is safe to spend today.

  • Updates as you spend
  • Warns you before money runs short

02 · Due radar

Nothing sneaks up on you.

Every bill, EMI and card due date on one calendar, with a heads-up before each one.

  • Bills, loans and cards in one place
  • Reminders before the due date

03 · Get out of EMIs sooner

See the day you are debt-free.

A payoff plan that shows when your last loan ends, and how a little extra each month brings that day closer.

  • Countdown to debt-free
  • See how much interest you save

04 · Can I afford it?

Know before you buy.

Check a purchase against the rest of your month before you pay, so a treat today never becomes a problem next week.

  • Instant yes, wait or no
  • Shows what it does to your month

05 · Monthly plan

Your month, planned for you.

A simple plan for the month, worked out on your phone from your income, bills and goals.

  • Spending, saving and payoff in one view
  • Adjusts as the month goes

WealthOn FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need to link my bank?

No. Import a statement file or add spends yourself. WealthOn never asks for bank logins or passwords.

Which banks work with statement import?

Any bank that gives you a PDF, Excel or CSV statement. Locked PDFs open on your phone, and the password is never saved.

Does it work outside India?

Yes. WealthOn works in any currency.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time ₹199 (about $3.99 elsewhere). Pay once, keep it.

How do I join the closed test?

Enter your Google Play email in the closed test section below. We add you to the test and email you the link, usually within a day.

How do I delete my data?

Everything stays on your phone. Go to Settings → Erase all data, or see Delete your data.
