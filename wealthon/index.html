What it does

Money, made simple

Built for people juggling a salary, bills and EMIs, with a short setup that doesn't wear you out.

Safe to spend

One clear number for what you can spend today, after bills and EMIs are covered.

Due radar

Every upcoming due date in one place, so nothing sneaks up on you.

Get out of EMIs sooner

A plan that shows how to close your loans earlier, and how much you save doing it.

Statement import

Import a bank statement and your spending gets sorted into categories for you.

Monthly plan

A simple plan for the month, with answers to the common money questions worked out on your phone.

Voice entry Coming soon
