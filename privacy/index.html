Website privacy

How forgeapps.in handles your data. Each app has its own privacy policy on its page.

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

This website uses no cookies and no ads. It counts visits anonymously so we can see what is useful, and if you write to us through the contact form, your message is emailed to us so we can reply. That's all.

Visit statistics

We use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, and may also use Cloudflare Web Analytics. They record things like:

  • which pages are viewed and which buttons are pressed (for example "Open on Google Play" or "Send feedback")
  • the site that linked you here, your country, and your device and browser type

They don't use cookies, don't store your IP address, and don't build a profile of you or follow you to other sites. The numbers are only seen in aggregate.

The contact form

When you send a message, the form sends your name (if given), email, the app and topic you chose, your message, the page you were on and your browser type to Web3Forms, which emails it to support@forgeapps.in. We use it only to reply to you, or to tell you when an app launches if you asked for that. We never sell it or add you to a mailing list. Web3Forms keeps a copy for a limited time under its own privacy policy.

Videos and fonts

YouTube videos load from youtube-nocookie.com, and only after you press play. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your browser's request like any website you visit.

Your choices

To have a message you sent deleted, or for any question about this page, email support@forgeapps.in.

The apps

Each app has its own privacy policy, terms and data-deletion steps. You'll find them on the app's page under Support.
