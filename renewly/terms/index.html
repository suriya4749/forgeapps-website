Renewly Terms of Use

Last updated 13 November 2026

These terms apply when you use the Renewly Android app, made by Forge Apps. By using Renewly you agree to them.

1. What Renewly is

Renewly keeps your family's documents (passports, IDs, licences, insurance policies, vehicle papers and more) in one private place on your phone, reads their expiry dates, and reminds you before they run out.

2. Reminders and dates

Renewly finds expiry dates automatically and reminds you on the dates you choose, but reading a document can sometimes be wrong and phones can delay or block notifications. Always check the dates Renewly shows against the document itself. You remain responsible for renewing your documents on time; Renewly is a helper, not an official record.

3. Your documents

Only add documents you are allowed to keep, and get the consent of family members (or be their guardian) before adding their documents. Your documents stay on your phone; keep your recovery key safe, because without it we cannot restore your vault on a new phone.

4. Price

5. Fair use

6. Availability
