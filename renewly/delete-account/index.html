Delete your Renewly data

Renewly by Forge Apps

Renewly has no accounts and keeps your data only on your phone (and, if you turned on sync, as an encrypted copy in your own Google Drive). We hold no copy, so deleting is in your hands and happens straight away.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Renewly.
  2. Go to Settings → Backup & sync → Erase all data.
  3. Confirm. Everything is removed from this phone immediately. If sync is on, you will be asked whether to also remove the encrypted copy from your Google Drive.

Option 2: without the app

  • On your phone: uninstall Renewly, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Renewly → Storage. This removes all its data from the phone.
  • Sync copy in Google Drive: open drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → Renewly → Delete hidden app data.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot delete it for you, but if you need help, email support@forgeapps.in with the subject "Delete my Renewly data" and we will walk you through it.

What gets deleted

  • All documents, photos and PDFs, their details, versions and checklists
  • Family members, vehicles, homes and emergency cards
  • Reminders, settings and the sharing log
  • The encrypted sync copy in your Google Drive, if you choose to remove it

What may be kept

  • Records of Renewly Plus purchases, which Google Play keeps under its own policies.
  • Copies you shared with others, and calendar events you exported, stay where you sent them; delete those there.
Data stored only on your phone: uninstalling Renewly, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes that data from your phone.
