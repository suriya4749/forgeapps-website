Renewly Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

Renewly has no accounts and no sign-in. The documents you scan or import, their details and your family list are stored only on your phone, encrypted. We (Forge Apps, the developer) never see them and keep no copy. Reading the expiry date from a scan happens on your phone. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share a document, turn on sync to your own Google Drive, or add renewals to your calendar. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

1. Information the app handles

WhatWhyWhere it is kept

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

2. Reading your documents

When you scan or import a document, Renewly reads its text on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition, to find the expiry date and document type. The images and text are not uploaded for this. You check the details before anything is saved.

3. Sync between phones (Renewly Plus, coming soon, off until you turn it on)

Sync is coming in an update. When it arrives and you turn it on, Renewly stores an encrypted copy of your vault in the hidden app-data area of your own Google Drive, so your other phones (and family phones you pair) can stay up to date. It is encrypted on your phone before upload with a key only your phones hold, so Google and we cannot read it. We do not run a server for this and receive nothing. Signing in to Google Drive is handled by Google under its own terms. You can turn sync off and remove the copy at any time in Settings → Backup & sync.

4. What else can leave your phone, only when you choose

  • Backup file: when you tap "Back up now", Renewly makes one encrypted file of your vault and lets you choose where to save it (for example your own Google Drive, email or a computer). It can only be opened with your 24-word recovery key. Where you keep it is up to you; we never receive it.
  • Recovery key: shown once in the app. You can save it as a PDF or share it to a place you choose; it is not copied to the clipboard and we never receive it.
  • Error reports: if the app runs into an error, it keeps a short log of the last errors on your phone (no document contents). Nothing is sent unless you tap "Send report", which opens an email you can read and edit before sending.
  • Safe sharing: when you share a document, Renewly makes a copy with a watermark (who it is for, why, and the date) and opens Android's share sheet. You pick where it goes; that copy is then handled by the app or person you sent it to.
  • Calendar: if you turn on "Add renewals to calendar", expiry and reminder dates are written to the calendar on your phone. If that calendar syncs to an online account, it is handled by that account's provider.
  • Feedback: if you send feedback, it goes from our website's contact form or your email app, with what you choose to write.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Play for downloading the app and for the Renewly Plus purchase.
  • Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition): runs on your phone; your documents are not sent to Google for this.
  • Google Drive, only if you turn on sync: stores the encrypted copy in your own account.

Renewly contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

6. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents, only while the scanner is open.
  • Notifications: for renewal reminders, worked out and scheduled on your phone.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on app lock; the fingerprint or face check is done by Android and Renewly never receives it.
  • Calendar: only if you turn on calendar export.
  • Internet: only for the Google Play purchase and, once available, sync.

7. How your data is protected

Your documents and details are stored in an encrypted database and encrypted files in Renewly's private storage, with the key kept in Android's secure Keystore. App lock adds a fingerprint, face or PIN check, and screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked by default. Your recovery key lets you restore your vault on a new phone; we do not have it and cannot recover it for you. Android's device backup is turned off for Renewly, so your vault is not copied to Google by it. The emergency card, if you fill it in, can be shown from the lock screen without unlocking: only put in it what you are happy for someone holding your phone to see.

8. How long we keep it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall Renewly. If you use sync, the encrypted copy stays in your Google Drive until you turn sync off and remove it, or delete it from your Google account. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to keep.

9. Your choices

  • Edit, rename or delete any document, person or detail in the app at any time.
  • Delete everything at once in Settings. See Delete your data.
  • Turn sync, calendar export, app lock and reminders on or off in Settings.

10. Children

Renewly is meant for adults managing their family's documents, including documents for their children. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change affects what leaves your phone, the app will tell you.

12. Contact

Renewly is made by Forge Apps. For privacy questions or requests, email support@forgeapps.in with "Renewly privacy" in the subject.
