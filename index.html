Process

From idea to your phone.

Four steps, repeated for every app. Scroll through how an idea becomes something on your home screen.

Step 01

Spot a real problem

We start from something people struggle with every day, not from a feature list.

  • Talk to real people
  • Write down the one job the app must do

Step 02

Design it simply

Screens are sketched, cut down and redrawn until the main task takes just a few taps.

  • One clear job per screen
  • Readable type and strong contrast

Step 03

Build and test

Real testers use it on their own phones in a closed test and tell us what to fix.

  • 14-day closed test on Google Play
  • Every report gets a reply

Step 04

Ship and keep shaping

It launches on Google Play, then keeps improving every month from your feedback.

  • Regular updates
  • Your ideas on the roadmap

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Who is behind Forge Apps?

    An independent studio in India that designs, builds and supports every app itself, from the first sketch to the replies in your inbox.

    Are your apps free?

    Every app is free to start. Where there is an upgrade, it is clearly priced and one-time wherever possible. WealthOn Pro, for example, is a one-time ₹199.

    Do your apps collect my data?

    We keep data on your phone wherever possible and never sell it. Each app has its own privacy policy on its page.

    How do I become a tester?

    Enter your Google Play email in the early access section. We add you to the test and email you the link, usually within a day.

    How do I delete my data?

    Each app’s page has a “Delete your data” link that explains how to do it in the app or by email.

    Can I suggest an app idea?

    Yes, please. Send it here. Every idea is read, and the best ones end up in the forge.
