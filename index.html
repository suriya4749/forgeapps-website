    FAQ

    Common questions

    How do I get help with an app?

    Use the contact form and pick the app, or email support@forgeapps.in. Each app's page also has a support section.

    Where is the privacy policy?

    Every app has its own privacy policy. Open the app's page and you'll find it in the Support section.

    How do I delete my data?

    Each app's page has a "Delete your data" link that explains how to do it in the app or by email.

    When will an app in testing launch?

    Apps in testing are being tried by real users first. Each app's page shows its stage, and the Google Play button appears as soon as it is live.

    Can I suggest an idea or a feature?

    Yes, please do. Send an idea; every suggestion is read.

    Questions or feedback?

    Feedback, a bug or an idea for the next app. Every message is read and answered.
