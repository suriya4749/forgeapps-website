Why Forge Apps exists.

Too many everyday apps want a sign-up, a bank login or your attention for ads. I wanted the opposite: small apps that do one job, keep your data on your phone and treat you like an adult.

So I started Forge Apps. The name comes from the forge, where rough metal is hammered until it is useful. That is how each app is made: start with a real problem, shape it simply, test it with real people, and keep hammering after launch.

How I work.

Mission and principlesForge Apps
  1. Mission01

    Useful before clever

    Every feature has to solve a real problem. If it doesn’t, it doesn’t ship.

  2. Privacy02

    Your data stays yours

    Apps keep data on your phone wherever possible and need no account. Each app has a plain-language privacy policy.

  3. Pricing03

    Fair and up front

    Free to start. Upgrades are one payment, priced in your currency, with no subscriptions sneaking in.

  4. Craft04

    Built with care

    Real testers before launch, steady updates after, and every message gets a reply from me.

What’s next.

More small helpers for everyday life: money, family documents, and the phone itself. Every new app goes to testers first, so if you want an early look, join the tester list or follow the updates.
