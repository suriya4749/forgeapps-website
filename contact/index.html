Quick answers.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within 1–2 days. Bug reports with a screenshot get fixed fastest: reply to our email with it.

Where do I report a bug?

Choose “Bug report” and the app above, and tell us what you tapped just before it happened.

How do I delete my data?

Each app has a “Delete your data” page with the steps. For anything else, write to us.
